Both visualizations utilize the same geospatial dataset and filtering options, allowing Supervisors to seamlessly switch between viewing individual waste reports and analyzing the spatial concentration of waste incidents without leaving the page.The Report Map focuses on operational monitoring by displaying the precise locations of individual reports, while the Heatmap provides an aggregated view of waste report density to identify hotspot areas.
