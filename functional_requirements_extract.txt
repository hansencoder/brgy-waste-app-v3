Its primary purpose is to introduce the system, provide essential public information, and encourage residents to register or log in. The page is designed to be informative yet simple, allowing both registered and non-registered users to access relevant information without exposing internal system functions.1. Navigation BarThe navigation bar provides quick access to the main sections of the website, including:HomeAbout the SystemGarbage Collection ScheduleLoginRegisterThis allows visitors to easily navigate the system before creating an account.2. Hero SectionThe hero section introduces the purpose of the system through a concise message, such as promoting proper waste management and community participation. It also contains two primary call-to-action buttons:Register – for new users who wish to create an account.Login – for existing users.This section immediately guides users toward the primary functions of the system.3. About the SystemThis section briefly explains:The purpose of the Waste Reporting System.The benefits of community participation in reporting waste-related concerns.How geospatial mapping and heatmap analysis help the barangay identify waste hotspots and improve decision-making.The content provides visitors with an understanding of why the system exists and how it benefits both residents and the barangay.4. Public AnnouncementsThe landing page displays announcements that have been marked by the Administrator as Public.Examples include:Changes to garbage collection schedules.Community clean-up drives.Waste segregation campaigns.Holiday collection advisories.Environmental awareness activities.Only announcements intended for the general public are shown. Announcements intended for registered users remain accessible only after logging in.5. Garbage Collection ScheduleThe current garbage collection schedule is displayed to both registered and non-registered users.The schedule includes:Collection dayCollection timeCovered puroks or service areasSpecial collection noticesProviding this information publicly helps residents stay informed and may reduce unnecessary waste reports caused by missed collection schedules.6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)A Frequently Asked Questions section provides answers to common inquiries, such as:How do I create an account?How do I submit a waste report?What types of waste can be reported?Why was my report marked as a duplicate?How can I support an existing report?How are hotspots generated on the map?This section helps users understand the system and reduces the need for direct assistance.7. Contact InformationThe landing page includes the barangay's official contact information, such as:Barangay office addressTelephone or mobile numberEmail address (if available)Office hoursThis enables residents to contact the barangay directly for concerns that cannot be addressed through the system.8. FooterThe footer provides supplementary information, including:Copyright noticeBarangay nameSystem version (optional)Quick links to Login and RegisterPrivacy Policy (optional)Terms of Use (optional)Overall ApproachThe landing page is intentionally designed to be informative rather than interactive. Instead of exposing operational features such as waste reporting or heatmap analysis, it focuses on introducing the system, communicating public information, and guiding users toward registration or login. This approach keeps the interface clean, ensures that only appropriate information is publicly accessible, and directs authenticated users to role-specific features after they sign in. It also supports transparency by making public announcements and collection schedules available to all residents while protecting internal functions and restricted announcements behind user authentication.Register Account ApproachThe Register Account page allows residents to create an account and gain access to the Waste Reporting System. The registration process is designed to be simple, secure, and user-friendly while ensuring that each account is associated with a verified contact information.1. Personal InformationThe user provides the following basic information:Full NamePositionThe Position identifies the user's designation within the barangay. For most users, this will be Resident, while official personnel such as the Barangay Captain, Barangay Secretary, Environmental Officer, Barangay Kagawad, or Waste Collection Coordinator may have their respective positions when their accounts are created by an Administrator.2. Contact InformationInstead of having separate fields for email and mobile number, the system uses a single Contact Information field.The user may enter either:Email AddressMobile NumberThe system automatically detects the input format and determines whether it is an email address or a mobile number.This approach simplifies the registration process while maintaining flexibility for users who prefer either method of communication.3. Password CreationThe user creates their account password by entering:PasswordConfirm PasswordThe system validates that:Both passwords match.The password satisfies the configured password policy (e.g., minimum length and complexity).Passwords are securely encrypted before being stored in the database.4. OTP VerificationAfter submitting the registration form, the system generates a One-Time Password (OTP) and sends it to the user's registered email address or mobile number.The user is redirected to the OTP Verification page, where they must:Enter the received OTP.Request a new OTP if the previous one expires.Correct their contact information if necessary before verification.The system validates the OTP based on its correctness and expiration period.5. Account CreationOnce the OTP is successfully verified:The account is automatically created.The contact information is marked as verified.The user can immediately log in using their registered contact information and password.No administrator approval is required, allowing residents to access the system without unnecessary delays.6. Duplicate Account ValidationBefore creating the account, the system checks whether the provided email address or mobile number is already registered.If the contact information already exists, the registration is rejected, and the user is informed that an account already exists for that contact information.This prevents duplicate accounts and ensures that each user has a unique identity within the system.7. Account SummaryDisplays:Full NameVerified Contact InformationAccount StatusQuick actions:Edit ProfileChange PasswordSubmit Waste Report ApproachThe Submit Waste Report module is the core feature of the Waste Reporting System. It enables residents to report waste-related concerns by providing the location of the incident, selecting standardized report information, and submitting supporting evidence. The reporting process is designed to be simple, consistent, and efficient while ensuring that the collected data is suitable for spatial analysis and decision-making.Before a report is accepted, the system performs several validation procedures, including barangay boundary validation, automatic purok identification, duplicate report detection, and report validation, to improve data quality and reduce redundant submissions.1. Accessing the Report FormResidents access the module by selecting Submit Waste Report from the Resident Dashboard.The system requests permission to access the device's location.If permission is granted, the system automatically detects the resident's current GPS location.If permission is denied, the resident may manually pin the report location on the interactive map.2. Report LocationThe resident identifies the location of the waste incident.The system provides:Interactive mapAutomatic GPS detectionManual map pinningAutomatic latitude and longitude retrievalOnce a location is selected, the system automatically performs geofence validation.If the selected location is outside the configured barangay boundary, the report cannot proceed.Example:The selected location is outside the barangay's service area. Please select a location within the official barangay boundary.If the location is valid, the system automatically determines the corresponding Purok using the configured polygon boundaries.Residents are not required to manually select their purok.3. Waste InformationAfter confirming the location, the resident provides the necessary report details.Waste Category (Required)The resident selects the type of waste concern.Examples include:Illegal DumpingOverflowing Garbage BinUncollected GarbageConstruction WasteYard WasteHazardous WasteOthersThe available categories are managed through the Waste Category Management module.Estimated Quantity (Required)The resident estimates the amount of waste using standardized options.Example:Small (1–2 garbage bags)Medium (3–5 garbage bags)Large (6–10 garbage bags)Very Large (More than 10 garbage bags)Using predefined options ensures consistency across reports and supports more meaningful statistical analysis.Waste Condition (Required)The resident selects the condition that best describes the reported waste.Examples include:Newly DumpedAccumulatingOverflowingScatteredBlocking DrainageBlocking RoadwayThese standardized conditions help the barangay assess the urgency and nature of the reported issue.Additional Remarks (Optional)Residents may provide additional information that cannot be expressed through the predefined fields.Examples include:"Beside the basketball court.""Located behind the blue gate.""Garbage has a foul odor."This field provides contextual information while keeping the reporting process primarily structured.Photo Attachment (Required or Optional)Residents upload one or more photographs of the waste.Whether photo submission is required or optional depends on the configuration set by the Administrator in the Report Settings module.Photographs assist in report verification and provide visual evidence of the reported concern.4. Automatic Duplicate DetectionOnce the location has been selected and the required information has been provided, the system automatically checks for existing reports using the configured:Duplicate detection radiusDuplicate detection time windowThe comparison considers:Distance between reportsWaste categoryReport statusDate and time submittedThis process helps reduce duplicate reports while still allowing residents to report genuinely different incidents.5. Nearby Existing ReportsIf similar reports are found, the system displays a popup containing anonymous nearby reports.The popup includes:Mini interactive mapResident's selected locationExisting report location(s)Approximate distanceWaste categoryReport statusDate reportedNumber of supportersNo personal information about the original reporter is displayed.Residents are presented with two options.Support Existing ReportIf the resident confirms that the existing report refers to the same issue:The resident's support is added to the existing report.The report's support count is increased.A new report is not created.The resident receives a confirmation that their support has been recorded.Continue New ReportIf the resident determines that the issue is different despite the nearby report:The popup is closed.The resident continues with the report submission process.This approach prevents unnecessary duplicate reports without restricting legitimate submissions.6. Report ValidationBefore the report is saved, the system validates:Required fieldsValid barangay locationValid waste categoryEstimated quantityWaste conditionFile typeMaximum upload sizeRate limitingDuplicate detection resultsIf any validation fails, appropriate error messages are displayed to allow the resident to correct the information.7. Report SubmissionAfter all validations have been successfully completed, the report is submitted.The system automatically:Generates a unique Report ID.Records the submission date and time.Stores the GPS coordinates.Associates the report with the logged-in resident.Assigns the detected Purok.Sets the initial report status to Pending Verification.Initializes the support count.Sends a notification confirming successful submission.8. Unlike the Administrator, who is responsible for daily operations and system management, the Supervisor uses the dashboard to monitor the overall status of waste-related concerns, evaluate operational performance, identify spatial trends, and support evidence-based decision-making. The dashboard is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of the system through summarized statistics, recent activities, interactive visualizations, and analytical insights without allowing direct modification of operational data.1. Active Hotspots OverviewThe dashboard presents a summarized list of locations currently identified as active waste hotspots based on heatmap analysis.Each hotspot summary displays:PurokNumber of ReportsDominant Waste CategoryHotspot Priority LevelSelecting View Heatmap redirects the Supervisor to the Heatmap Analysis page for detailed spatial analysis.5. Collection Schedule OverviewThe dashboard displays the next scheduled waste collection activities.Displayed information includes:Collection DayCollection TimeCovered Purok(s)Special Collection NotesThis enables the Supervisor to verify whether collection schedules align with current reporting trends.6.
