Unlike the Administrator, who is responsible for daily operations and system management, the Supervisor uses the dashboard to monitor the overall status of waste-related concerns, evaluate operational performance, identify spatial trends, and support evidence-based decision-making. The dashboard is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of the system through summarized statistics, recent activities, interactive visualizations, and analytical insights without allowing direct modification of operational data.1. Summary CardsThe upper section of the dashboard presents key performance indicators (KPIs) through summary cards, enabling the Supervisor to quickly assess the current operational status of the barangay.The dashboard displays:Total Reports – Total number of waste reports submitted.Pending Verification – Reports awaiting verification by the Administrator.Verified Reports – Reports confirmed as valid.In Progress Reports – Reports currently being addressed.Resolved Reports – Reports successfully completed.Active Hotspots – Areas currently identified as waste hotspots based on heatmap analysis.Total Support Count – Total number of resident supports across all reports.Reports Submitted Today – Number of reports received on the current day.Each summary card may be selected to navigate to the corresponding monitoring page for more detailed information.2.
