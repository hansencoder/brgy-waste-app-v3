Unlike the Administrator, who is responsible for daily operations and system management, the Supervisor uses the dashboard to monitor the overall status of waste-related concerns, evaluate operational performance, identify spatial trends, and support evidence-based decision-making. The dashboard is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of the system through summarized statistics, recent activities, interactive visualizations, and analytical insights without allowing direct modification of operational data.1.
